Hands-on laptop tests

Works offline in Edge or Chrome. Allow camera/microphone when asked. Turn the volume to about half before the speaker test.

1. Screen: dead pixels, backlight, colours

The screen fills with one colour at a time. Click, or press Space / arrow keys, to go to the next colour. Esc to exit. Look closely for dots that stay dark or bright, glowing edges on black, yellow patches, lines or flicker. Change the brightness on each colour.

2. Keyboard

Press every key. Keys turn green once they work. Also test Fn + brightness / volume / keyboard-backlight keys (Windows handles those, so watch the screen). A blue outline means the key is held down; keys that stay outlined are stuck.

3. Touchpad / mouse

Inside the box: left click, right click, double-click, scroll with two fingers both ways, drag. Press the physical buttons (or the bottom corners) too.

4. Touch screen & pen (if it has one)

Draw lines across the whole box, especially the corners and edges, with one finger then several fingers. Gaps in the lines = dead zones. Marks appearing by themselves = "ghost touch" (faulty digitizer).

5. Speakers & headphone jack

Check sound comes out of the correct side, with no crackling or buzzing. The sweep goes from very low to very high pitch: listen for rattles or distortion. Repeat with headphones plugged in.

6. Microphone

Talk normally: the bar should move. Then record 5 seconds and play it back. It should sound clear, without hiss or crackle.

7. Webcam

Check the image is sharp, the colours look right, there are no lines or spots, and the camera light comes on. If there are two cameras (e.g. a Windows Hello IR camera), pick each one.
